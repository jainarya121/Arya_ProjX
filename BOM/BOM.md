## Component choice: motor driver

**TB6612FNG chosen over the L298N in Appendix A.** It is more efficient and more compact than the L298N, but it needs a regulated motor rail at 9.0 V: its VM must not be connected straight to the 3S LiPo. The supply limits behind this are in [Task 2](../docs/task2_datasheet.md).
